# Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 22, 2025

Governing Law: Kenya

1. Introduction  
Codelink values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your data when using our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect  
Codelink collects information such as your name, phone number, password, location data during trips, and emergency alerts. All authentication and data storage are managed securely through Google Firebase.

3. How We Use Your Data  
- To verify your account via Firebase Authentication.
- To link users securely using 4-digit verification codes.
- To log trips and maintain service records.
- To handle lost and found reports.
- To send emergency alerts and safety notifications.
- To improve service performance and reliability.

4. Data Storage and Security  
All user data is encrypted and stored in Firebase servers. Access is restricted to authorized admins only. We apply strong encryption protocols and regular audits to protect data integrity.

5. Data Sharing  
Codelink does not sell or share user data with third parties. Data may only be shared when required by law or for public safety.

6. User Rights  
You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data. Requests can be made through the App or by contacting us directly.

7. Account Deletion and Retention  
Deleted accounts are permanently removed from Firebase servers after admin approval. Some data may be retained for legal compliance.

8. Updates to This Policy  
Codelink may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes.
